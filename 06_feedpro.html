06 Mechanical Architecture

High-capacity pellet hopper feeding into a high-RPM pneumatic blower. Features a motorized variable-width deflector nozzle for dynamic spread control (close bank vs far center-pond broadcasting).

07 Materials & Finishes

IP67 food-grade HDPE hopper, Marine-grade 316L stainless steel broadcasting manifold, powder-coated carbon steel frame, UV-stable polycarbonate inspection windows

11 Engineering Risks & Unknowns

  • Pellet crush/dust accumulating in blower housing
  • Spread actuator binding due to moisture/salt
  • Motor overheating during prolonged far-cast broadcasting
  • Water ingress from splashing during pond aeration

12 Manufacturing Notes

HDPE hoppers rotomolded (food-grade), marine-grade stainless hardware, powder-coat steel frame, IP67 cable glands on all penetrations to withstand splash zones.

13 Maintenance & Serviceability

Air intake requires regular filter clearing to prevent blower stalling. Marine-grade components used to prevent rusting, but actuator hinges require monthly lubrication.
